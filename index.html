01

Operational intelligence

Turning KPI noise into targeted service improvements

Problem

Recurring operational issues were affecting service quality, but the highest-risk patterns were difficult to isolate across a broad KPI set.

Approach

02

Process design

Rebuilding an escalation system for scale

Workflow design · Jira taxonomy · Dashboards

Problem

A 100+ case backlog, inconsistent routing and a single-owner escalation path were slowing investigation and creating avoidable dependency.

Approach

Built the triage workflow, introduced a consistent Jira taxonomy, redesigned escalation into a two-tier model and created the team's first operating playbooks and visibility dashboards.

Impact

Stabilised the queue, removed a single point of failure and helped reduce new-hire ramp time by 25%.

03

Risk & quality

Challenging a costly false-positive fraud signal

Investigation · Policy analysis · Risk judgement

Problem

A group of AON insurance policies had been flagged as potentially fraudulent, creating the risk of incorrect action and substantial financial loss.

Approach

Investigated the underlying policy pattern, validated it against the relevant U.S. temporary-driver insurance context and surfaced the false-positive assessment.

Impact

Prevented an estimated $200–350K in wrongful losses while maintaining 99% quality performance.
